This installs ICG, the social-media comment Overlay tool, into the Hosts you choose on the next step:

  • Premiere Pro (23 and newer): a panel under Window > Extensions > ICG.
  • DaVinci Resolve Studio (19 and newer): a plugin under Workspace > Workflow Integrations > ICG. DaVinci Resolve (free) cannot load plugins; use the web app instead.

Quit Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve before continuing. An uninstaller is placed in /Library/Application Support/ICG/.
